The Origin Story

Why D.I.C.E. Exists

🍽️

The Dinner Table Moment

It started with a random thought during dinner. iPhone has Siri. Samsung has Galaxy AI. Google has Gemini Live. But what about laptops — where computing actually happens at its deepest level?

No company has legitimately cracked this. Laptops are where developers build, creators create, and students learn — and yet there's no truly powerful AI assistant built for them.

So the thought was simple: "Let's make Siri for laptops." Then it became — "No, let's make something better than Siri. Better than OK Google. And give it away for free."

That dinner table thought became weeks of sleepless nights, study schedules bent around passion, and a product that's now days away from public release.
