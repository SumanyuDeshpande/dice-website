Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about D.I.C.E. before it launches.

Is D.I.C.E. really free?

Yes — completely, unconditionally, forever free. No subscriptions, no hidden charges, no "premium tier". D.I.C.E. was built on the philosophy that powerful tools should be accessible to everyone. That's not a marketing line — it's the entire point.

What Windows versions does D.I.C.E. support?

D.I.C.E. currently supports Windows 11 only. This ensures the best performance and deepest system integration possible. Support for other platforms is something we're thinking about for the future as D.I.C.E. grows.

Does D.I.C.E. work offline?

Partially. Many core commands — like opening apps, managing files, controlling system settings — work completely offline. However, some AI-powered features that require real-time data or cloud intelligence do need an internet connection. Think of it as: your machine's controls are always yours, even offline.

Is my data safe? Does D.I.C.E. send anything to the cloud?

Your privacy is a core design principle of D.I.C.E. All command processing happens locally on your device — your voice, your commands, your data never leave your machine unless a specific feature explicitly requires internet (like a web search command). No surveillance, no data harvesting, ever.

When is D.I.C.E. releasing?

D.I.C.E. is currently being pushed further to ensure it launches at its absolute best. The new release window is Mid to End of June 2026. Join the waitlist above to get notified the exact moment it drops. Good things take time. 🎲

Would D.I.C.E. be releasing for Mac?

Siri is doing pretty good in Mac OS. Do you want it in Mac also? — Provide your suggestion in the feedback form given on the page. 🎲

How is D.I.C.E. different from OK Google or Cortana?

OK Google and Cortana are passive assistants — they answer questions and do simple tasks. D.I.C.E. is an execution engine. It understands context, runs complex multi-step commands, integrates deeply with your system, works for developers and power users, processes locally, and gives you control that no other assistant does. Oh, and it's free. They aren't.

Will there be a mobile version?

A mobile version is in the pipeline — but not anytime soon. D.I.C.E. is built for laptops first, where it's needed most. Once D.I.C.E. grows and establishes itself on desktop, expanding to mobile is absolutely on the horizon. Stay tuned. 🚀

Can I suggest features or report issues?

Absolutely — and we genuinely want you to. Use the Feedback form on this page. Every message goes directly to the founder and is read personally. D.I.C.E. will be shaped by its community — your voice matters here.

Do I need to create an account to use D.I.C.E.?

No account. No login. No sign-up. Download, install, and you're in control instantly. D.I.C.E. believes your tools shouldn't require you to hand over your identity just to use them.
